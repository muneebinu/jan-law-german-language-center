What German level should you start with?

German courses are commonly organised by CEFR levels. A1 is the beginner level, A2 builds on basic knowledge, and B1 develops more independent communication. Choose a course that matches what you can already understand and say, rather than selecting a level only because of its name.

What to check before joining a German academy

  • Confirm that the course level matches your current German ability.
  • Ask what topics and language skills are covered.
  • Check the estimated duration, total fee and any separate material costs.
  • Confirm the physical class location and current timetable.
  • Ask what kind of assessment or progress feedback is included.
  • Do not assume that a language course is an official examination centre or guarantees a visa, job or university admission.
