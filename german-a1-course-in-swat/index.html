What you learn in German A1

The course develops practical German skills through lessons and practice across reading, listening, writing and speaking. Main learning areas include:

  • German alphabet, pronunciation, greetings and introductions
  • Personal details, family, friends and describing people
  • Numbers, dates, days, months, time and appointments
  • Food, drinks, shopping, prices, home and daily routines
  • Basic present-tense verbs, personal pronouns, articles and simple questions

Who should join German A1?

A1 is suitable for complete beginners who want to start learning German in Swat. You do not need previous German knowledge to begin.

Learning outcomes

  • Introduce yourself and share basic personal information
  • Understand and use simple everyday expressions
  • Ask and answer basic questions
  • Build short sentences about familiar topics
