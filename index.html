Your learning journey

A1 → A2 → B1. Progress with Purpose.

Start at the level that matches your current knowledge and build your German step by step.

A1

Build the basics

Learn to introduce yourself, understand simple expressions and communicate about basic needs.

A2

Grow your confidence

Communicate about familiar routines and situations with a wider range of vocabulary.

B1

Communicate independently

Work towards expressing opinions, describing experiences and handling common situations.

Moving to the next level depends on your existing knowledge and learning progress.

Frequently asked questions

Questions Before You Begin?

Here are answers to common questions. If a detail is not confirmed below, contact us directly.

Do I need previous German knowledge to join A1?

No. A1 is the beginner level and is suitable if you are starting German from the beginning.

How much do the courses cost?

A1 is PKR 35,000, A2 is PKR 40,000 and B1 is PKR 50,000.

How long does each course take?

A1 takes approximately 1.5 months, A2 approximately 1.5 months and B1 approximately 2–2.5 months depending on the student.

Where is the academy located?

Dherai, Tehsil Kabal, Swat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan — next to Zubair Hospital, opposite Dherai Ground.

Are classes physical or online?

Jan Law German Language Center is presented as a classroom-based center in Dherai. Contact us to confirm current class arrangements and batch availability.

Is exam preparation available?

Please contact Jan Law to confirm current exam-preparation options and the specific exam you are planning to take.

Are certificates provided?

Certificate arrangements have not been confirmed on this website. Please ask the center directly before enrolling.

When does the next batch start?

Batch start dates can change. Contact Jan Law on WhatsApp for the next available intake.

How can I enrol?

Complete the enquiry form on this page to prepare a WhatsApp message, or contact the center directly at +92 318 9150911.

How can I contact Jan Law?

WhatsApp or call +92 318 9150911. You can also visit the center next to Zubair Hospital, opposite Dherai Ground.
