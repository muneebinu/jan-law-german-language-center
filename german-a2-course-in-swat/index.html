What you learn in German A2

The course develops practical German skills through lessons and practice across reading, listening, writing and speaking. Main learning areas include:

  • Everyday conversations about home, work, study and free time
  • Describing past events, plans, preferences and experiences
  • Expanding vocabulary for travel, shopping, health and daily needs
  • Developing sentence structure, word order and common connectors
  • Practising reading, listening, writing and speaking in familiar situations

Who should join German A2?

A2 is intended for learners who have already developed basic German and want to progress beyond beginner communication. If you are unsure whether your current level is suitable, contact the centre before enrolling.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand frequently used expressions on familiar topics
  • Describe routines, experiences and simple plans
  • Communicate in common everyday situations
  • Write and understand short, straightforward messages
