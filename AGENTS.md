# Jan Law website project guidance

Brand: Jan Law German Language Center. Tagline: A Path to Excellence.
Location: Dherai, Tehsil Kabal, Swat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan.
Landmark: next to Zubair Hospital, opposite Dherai Ground.
Courses: German A1, A2 and B1.
Palette: #172b35, #fff8ee, #f26a1b, #ffc83d, #dcefff.

Do not invent course fees, contact numbers, email, opening hours, testimonials, accreditations, exam-center status or visa/job guarantees. Ask the owner for confirmation.

Design direction: premium editorial typography, accessible contrast, semantic HTML, responsive mobile-first layouts, keyboard support, visible focus, and reduced-motion support. Avoid generic gradients and fake reviews.
