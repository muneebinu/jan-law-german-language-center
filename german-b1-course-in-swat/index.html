What you learn in German B1

The course develops practical German skills through lessons and practice across reading, listening, writing and speaking. Main learning areas include:

  • Talking about personal experiences, plans, interests and familiar events
  • Expressing opinions and giving reasons
  • Describing situations, problems and possible solutions
  • Building longer sentences with connectors and subordinate clauses
  • Practising reading, listening, writing and speaking around everyday topics

Who should join German B1?

B1 is suitable for learners who have already developed basic and elementary German and want to communicate with more independence. Contact Jan Law if you need help deciding whether your current level is ready for B1.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain opinions and support them with reasons
  • Describe experiences, events and future plans
  • Understand the main points of clear everyday communication
  • Write connected text about familiar subjects
